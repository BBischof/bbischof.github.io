How to read the charts
  • Stop duration: the scale ends at 120s; arrows label longer stops.
  • Reference lines: dashed = average; dotted = ±1 standard deviation. Stop-duration references exclude rest stops. The compact moving-average panel has its own seconds scale.
  • Moving average: the previous 10 ordinary stops, excluding the current stop. Starts after 10 are available.
  • Bike: the bike used to arrive at the selected station. Each timeline segment spans consecutive sections on the same bike; hover to identify short segments.
  • Rest stops: gold rules connect matching diamonds across the charts; hover for duration. Excluded from docking and travel averages; ordinary travel pauses stay included.
  • Average speed: the mean of the individual section speeds, so short and long sections count equally.
